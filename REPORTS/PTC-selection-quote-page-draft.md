# High Voltage Coolant Heater Selection and Quotation

This page does two things: it helps you select the right high voltage coolant heater, and it tells you exactly what to send us so we can quote it. No catalogue guessing, no back-and-forth over missing numbers.

## Select in four steps

**1. Heat load.** Tell us the lowest ambient the vehicle must operate in, the target coolant temperature, and how long the heater has to hold it. Our power bands are 6 to 18 kW, 20 to 25 kW and 30 to 35 kW. With the duty stated, the band picks itself.

**2. Voltage platform.** Our heaters cover 400V, 600V and 800V platforms, and the Q series runs to 1100V. Send the full min/max DC voltage range at the heater terminals, not just the nominal voltage.

**3. Coolant loop.** State the coolant type and concentration, the inlet temperature range, and the flow rate. A heater can only deliver the power the loop can carry away, so the flow figure is not optional.

**4. Control interface.** The A and Q series take CAN plus an enable signal, so the vehicle controller commands them. The E series runs on a thermostat with no CAN. Tell us what control the vehicle provides.

For the full reasoning behind each step, read the selection guide and the sizing article. For the product range, see the HVCH product page.

## The interface record

Send us this filled in and we can quote without a round of questions.

| Item | What to write down |
|---|---|
| Vehicle and application | Bus, truck, construction vehicle; battery preheat, cabin heat, or both |
| Voltage platform | Nominal voltage and the full min/max DC range |
| Power band | Heat load, lowest ambient, target coolant temperature, hold time |
| Coolant | Type and concentration, inlet temperature range, flow rate |
| Control | CAN + enable, or thermostat |
| Installation | Space, mounting, coolant connections, low-voltage supply (12V/24V) |
| Volume and samples | Annual volume, sample plan, target date |

## What happens after you send an enquiry

We confirm the series and power band from your interface record, then quote per project. Pricing is per project; there is no list price and no online ordering. Samples are validated in the real loop before the configuration is frozen for production. Share your application and the interface record and we will come back with a quotation and a sample plan.
