## 英文正文
Three thermal control functions. One integrated unit.

EVLINK’s Three-in-One Thermal Management Controller combines the compressor controller, refrigeration system ECU and high-voltage PTC controller for 600 V / 800 V commercial-vehicle platforms.

The control logic links compressor speed to system water temperature, expansion valve opening to superheat, and condenser fan operation to high-side pressure. Fan thresholds and speed curves are calibrated for the project.

Using your own compressor? Adaptation requires its technical and motor parameters. CAN protocol, IDs, baud rate and control logic can also be customized for the vehicle program.
