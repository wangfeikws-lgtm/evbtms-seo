

The question that opens every serious conversation about our three-in-one controller:

"Can it work with our own compressor?"

Short answer: yes. The details are where projects go wrong.

Most bus and truck programs already have a qualified compressor, and nobody wants to re-qualify one. Send us your compressor's technical and motor parameters; we match the DCAC section to your unit. The controller fits your system, not the reverse.

Three loops, each reading a real signal:

One, compressor speed follows water temperature against the target: rises, speed rises; drops, speed drops.

Two, expansion valve follows superheat: high superheat, wider opening; low superheat, narrower.

Three, condenser fan follows high-side pressure: starts around 13 bar, calibrated per project.

One box, three controllers: DCAC compressor drive, refrigeration ECU, HV PTC. One unit instead of three.

Scoping a program? Send vehicle platform, voltage, compressor parameters - that's what I need to assess fit.
