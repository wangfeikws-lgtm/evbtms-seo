Somebody asked me this week: "What do you need from me to quote your three-in-one controller?"

Most first messages we receive are "please send your price list." We can send one. It answers nothing — the wrong voltage variant costs a second round of samples, and the right answer was never about price.

The quote that matters is a technical fit answer. It starts with three lines of project facts.

One: platform and voltage. 600V or 800V? Our controller runs two input windows, 250–750V DC and 600–1000V DC. One line decides the variant.

Two: the compressor. Ours or yours? 5–10 kW adjustable on our side. If it's your compressor, send the technical and motor parameters — we adapt the control to it.

Three: envelope and bus. Roughly 280×200×95 mm, vertical mounting, -40 to +65°C, CAN 2.0 with protocol, ID, baud rate and control logic matched to your vehicle network per project. That tells us it bolts into your bay and speaks your network.

Send those three and you get back which variant fits, what adapts, and what's still open — before anyone talks price. MOQ starts at 10 units; conventional lead time around 15 days.

That's the whole intake. Three lines, one technical answer.
