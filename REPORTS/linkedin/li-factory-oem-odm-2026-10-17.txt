Buyers touring suppliers in China ask the same first question: who actually builds the heater?

A trading company and a manufacturer answer an RFQ very differently. When the spec changes mid-project, one requotes. The other opens the design file.

Our answer: EVLINK was founded in 2020. We run an 8,000㎡ production base where PTC electric-heating R&D and manufacturing sit under the same roof.

Three things I would check when qualifying one:

First, R&D on site. When development and production share a building, a parameter change goes from drawing to sample without a middleman.

Second, duty cycles that match yours. Our heaters sit in high-voltage air and coolant lines on electric buses, mining trucks, and commercial vehicles. Heavy duty is daily business.

Third, who their customers are. Ours are OEMs, vehicle and equipment developers, and system integrators. You learn to read drawings like a partner when the other side holds the drawing standards.

One figure from our internal queue could not be verified in published materials, so it stays out. Every number here is verified.

Qualifying a supplier? Bring vehicle type, voltage platform, heater type, required power, operating environment, and target volumes.
