## 英文旁白与分镜
### 0–15秒：开场
Choosing coolant for a cold-climate battery thermal management system? Freeze protection is one requirement. Here are four checks to discuss with your suppliers before you order.
画面：真实BTMS全景与细节，四项检查图标。

### 15–35秒：工况
First, define your operating conditions. Include storage temperatures, cold-start conditions, and the required operating range. Confirm the approved coolant specification and concentration for your system.
画面：冬季工况示意；Storage / Cold start / Operating range。不填未经证实的温度、浓度或性能数值。

### 35–55秒：完整回路
Second, check the complete loop. Review coolant compatibility with hoses, seals, pumps, and heat exchangers. Ask for compatibility information covering the selected fluid and your system configuration.
画面：Conceptual diagram，依次高亮组件；无真实配置图时不画型号专属接口与连接。

### 55–80秒：低温验证
Third, request cold-condition validation. Ask how the selected coolant, pump, and circuit will meet your flow and thermal requirements during a cold start. Request evidence relevant to your configuration.
画面：Flow requirements / Thermal requirements / Configuration-specific evidence。无报告时只展示清单，不显示实测曲线、测试数值或通过标记。

### 80–100秒：维护
Fourth, agree on filling and maintenance requirements. Confirm dilution-water requirements, inspection intervals, and replacement procedures. Ask the fluid supplier before mixing different coolant products.
画面：维护清单；操作流程未经技术确认前不演示加液、排气或拆装。

### 100–115秒：可选HVCH
If your circuit includes a high-voltage coolant heater, confirm its coolant requirements as well. Review the complete configuration before purchasing.
画面：真实HVCH图，标注If included in your circuit；无HVCH素材时用文字卡，不生成冒充EVLINK产品的替代图。

画面：真实产品、evbtms.com、Read the guide / Discuss your application。
