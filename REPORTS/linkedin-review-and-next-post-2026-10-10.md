## 下一篇英文正文（原创改写 v1）

Evaluating a three-in-one thermal management controller for an electric bus or commercial EV?

Start with the control brief—not just the voltage and power ratings.

EVLINK’s three-in-one controller combines the compressor controller, refrigeration-system ECU and high-voltage PTC controller. For the cooling functions, three different signals matter:

• Compressor speed: system water temperature and the target temperature.
• Expansion-valve opening: refrigerant superheat.
• Condenser-fan control: high-side pressure, with project-specific switching points and speed curves.

Before sample evaluation, provide the compressor’s technical and motor parameters, your system targets and CAN requirements. EVLINK supports project-specific compressor adaptation and customization of CAN protocol, IDs, baud rate and control logic, subject to reviewing the project inputs.

When assessing integration costs, compare the actual controller, harness and interface requirements. A smaller component count is not a substitute for a validated system design.

Planning an integration review? Contact Peter Wang on LinkedIn with your non-confidential application requirements. Keep confidential drawings out of public comments.
